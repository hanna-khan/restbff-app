About REST Foundation

Building a financially inclusive world, one community at a time

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and sustainable microfinance services that empower underserved individuals and communities to achieve economic independence, build resilient livelihoods, and create lasting positive change. We are committed to breaking the cycle of poverty through financial inclusion, education, and unwavering support.

Our Vision

A world where every individual, regardless of their economic background, has access to the financial tools and resources needed to pursue their dreams, build sustainable businesses, and contribute to thriving communities. We envision a future where financial exclusion is eliminated and opportunity is universal.

Our Story

REST Foundation was established in 2010 by a group of passionate financial professionals and community development experts who witnessed firsthand the transformative power of microfinance. Inspired by the success of microfinance pioneers around the world, they set out to create an organization that would bring these life-changing services to underserved communities.

Starting with just 50 clients and $100,000 in capital, REST Foundation has grown into a leading microfinance institution serving over 50,000 active clients across 150 communities. Our growth has been driven by our unwavering commitment to our clients' success and our innovative approach to financial inclusion.

Today, we offer a comprehensive suite of microfinance products and services, including microloans, savings programs, insurance products, and financial literacy training. Our 98% loan repayment rate and countless success stories demonstrate the effectiveness of our approach and the resilience of our clients.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Empowerment

We believe in empowering individuals to take control of their financial futures through education, support, and access to resources.

Integrity

We operate with the highest ethical standards, ensuring transparency, honesty, and accountability in all our interactions.

Community

We recognize that strong communities are built on mutual support and collaboration, and we foster these connections.

Innovation

We continuously seek innovative solutions to better serve our clients and overcome barriers to financial inclusion.

Sustainability

We are committed to creating sustainable solutions that benefit both people and the planet for generations to come.

Our Impact

Making a real difference in people's lives

Economic Empowerment

Our clients have created over 75,000 jobs in their communities, with average income increases of 150% within two years of receiving their first loan.

Women's Advancement

70% of our clients are women entrepreneurs who are now financially independent and empowering other women in their communities.

Health Education

Distributing bilingual health flyers and promoting preventive healthcare through family-centered initiatives.

Community Development

Our clients have reinvested over $10 million back into their local communities through various development initiatives.
